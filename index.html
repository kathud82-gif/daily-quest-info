The Daily Quest

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026. Covers versions 1.2.1 and 1.3; data-clearing controls are available from version 1.3.

Who we are

The Daily Quest is a children's learning and puzzle app operated by Kate Hudson, trading as The Daily Quest. Kate Hudson is responsible for personal information handled in connection with the app and support enquiries.

For support or privacy questions, contact thedailyquestapp@gmail.com.

Information saved by the app

The app saves an optional nickname, the selected age group, game answers, found words, stars, rewards and optional reminder settings on your device. This information personalises the greeting, remembers progress and provides reminders.

No account is required. We encourage children to use a made-up nickname and avoid entering a full name or other personal details.

Information sent to us

The app does not send the nickname, age-group choice, game progress or reminder settings to us. We cannot access that information on your device.

The app does not include advertising, third-party usage analytics, chat or in-app purchases. Game content is included in the app and can be played offline.

We do not sell personal information.

Optional reminders

Daily reminders are switched off initially. A grown-up can enable them and choose a time. The app asks for the device's notification permission when reminders are enabled.

Reminders are scheduled on the device; they are not sent from our server. You can switch them off in the app or through the device's settings.

Removing saved information

Saved information remains on your device until you clear it or remove the app.

From version 1.3, grown-up controls let you clear the nickname, game progress, stars, rewards and reminder settings, and cancel scheduled reminders. These controls are not available in version 1.2.1. In that version, you can clear the nickname, switch off reminders and delete the app to remove its local saved information.

Device backups may retain copies according to your Apple or Google backup settings. Clearing information inside the app does not delete separate backups. We cannot recover locally saved progress.

Contacting us and email retention

If a parent or guardian emails us, we receive their email address and the information included in their message. Please avoid sending children's personal information or screenshots containing it.

We use correspondence to answer enquiries, resolve problems and handle privacy requests. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in providing support and managing the app, where that interest is not overridden by your rights. We may also process correspondence where necessary to meet a legal obligation.

We normally delete routine support correspondence within 12 months after an enquiry is resolved. We may retain specific correspondence longer where necessary to handle an ongoing complaint or meet a legal obligation.

We use a standard free Gmail account to handle correspondence. Google processes information as part of providing Gmail and may process it outside the UK. Google's Privacy Policy explains its processing: Google’s Privacy Policy.

Apple, Google and TestFlight

Apple and Google separately operate their stores, device services and backup services under their own privacy policies.

During TestFlight testing, Apple may process installation information, diagnostics and feedback. If you submit a screenshot or feedback, check that it does not include personal information you do not wish to share. Information you choose to send through TestFlight is separate from the game information stored locally by the app.

Your privacy rights

Where UK data protection law applies, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold, restrict its use, object to processing, or request portability where applicable. Some rights depend on the circumstances and lawful basis for processing.

To make a request, email thedailyquestapp@gmail.com. We cannot retrieve game information stored only on your device; you can remove it as described above.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or information practices change. We will update the date above when we do.
